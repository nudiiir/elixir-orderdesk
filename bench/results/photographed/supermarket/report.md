# What Muse Spark identified: supermarket / FMCG

Catalogue names are French food and household names, usually brand first then type and variant: BISCUIT SANDWICH, YAOURT AROMATISE, CONFITURE ABRICOT, HARISSA, MOUTARDE. Sizes are weights and volumes glued to the number: 400G, 1KG, 1.8KG, 1L, 75CL, 125ML, 180ML, 500ML, plus 12W and 7W for lamps. Packs are counts with P or X: 3P, 6P, 8P, 14P, 16P, X4, 9X24, 120P. Two products of one brand differ only by variant fruit or flavour, form or recipe, and size or pack: KOOL BREAK vs KOOL BREAK WHITE, CONFITURE ABRICOT vs FRAISE, FOURRE vs ENROBE vs SANDWICH, 400G vs 800G vs 1KG.
