# What Muse Spark identified: pharmacy / medicines

Catalogue writes BRAND + strength + galenic form + pack: e.g. 10MG CP PELLI SEC B/30, 250MG/5ML SP FL 60ML, 0.1% COLLYRE FL 5ML. Strengths use MG/G/MCG/MUI/UI/% with comma decimals, single (10MG) or double/triple (500MG/65MG, 500/62.5MG, 1G/125MG, 0.2G/0.5G/100ML). Forms are abbreviated: CP, GLES/GELS, SP, SUSP BUV, SOL, GTTES, PDRE SACHT, AMP, INJ, SUPP, OVUL, CREME/GEL/PDE/T. Packs are B/XX, BT, S/30, T/15G for tubes and FL XXML volumes. Two products of one brand differ by dosage, form/pack and AD/ENF or LP.
