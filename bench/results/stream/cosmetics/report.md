# What Muse Spark identified: pharmacy and parapharmacy

Catalogue names start with brand then function, often abbreviated: BAD for toothbrush, BIB for biberon, SHAMP for shampoo, DENT for dentifrice, CP for comprime. Sizes are volumes and masses glued with no space (250ML, 50G, 400MG, 0.05%) and bandages write length x width (4M X 10CM, 10CM X 4M). Packs are coded B/10, B/20, B/30, P/72, C/120, SACHET, BOITE and codes like S 2032, SP 752, YC 7889. Two products of one brand differ by dosage, volume, flavour or scent, colour, size or population (BEBE / ENFANT / ADULTE) and pack count.
